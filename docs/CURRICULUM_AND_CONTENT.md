# Curriculum and content governance

## Source of truth
Obtain and review the current official syllabus applicable to the programme/cohort at RIPER before populating course data. The legacy HTML files are not authoritative curriculum sources.

## Verification record
For every curriculum version, record programme/cohort, issuing institution/authority, document title/version/date, source URL or provenance, date checked, reviewer/status, and unresolved ambiguity.

Never infer a subject title, code, credit value, semester mapping, unit heading, or effective date.

## Resource publication checklist
Confirm correct subject/unit mapping, title/type, language, author/source, URL/file path, copyright/licence or permission, file readability, accessibility where practical, reviewer and last-checked date, and absence of patient/student/confidential data.

## Status values
- `draft`: not public.
- `needs-review`: awaiting academic/content review.
- `verified`: checked against its stated source.
- `published`: approved for public display.
- `archived`: hidden from default discovery with a correction/history note.

Only content that is suitable to share and approved for publication should appear in the live catalogue.

## Copyright and safety
Do not mirror copyrighted textbooks, paid coaching material, restricted institutional files, or third-party PDFs without permission. Prefer official/open resources and link to lawful source pages. A syllabus listing a book does not grant redistribution rights. Do not imply RIPER endorsement without authorization.
