Privacy

Short version: students are anonymous, and there is nothing to collect.

What we collect

Nothing about students. There are no student accounts, no sign-in, no tracking cookies and no analytics on this site. Reading the library leaves no trace beyond ordinary web-server logs.

What is held

  • Staff accounts — name, email and a hashed password, so staff can sign in. Passwords are hashed (PBKDF2) and never stored in plain text.
  • Content and its history — the material, plus an audit trail of who changed what.
  • Standard server logs — IP, path and time, kept by the host for security only.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, ads or trackers.
  • No third-party CDN, fonts or scripts — everything is served from this site.
  • No names, phone numbers, marks, attendance or any student record.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Material

Study material is kept in a shared Google Drive folder; Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that folder. This site stores only links to it.

Questions

To ask about or correct anything held, use the contact page.
