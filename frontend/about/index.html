About this library

The RIPER e-Library is a free, student-run study library for B.Pharm students. Its job is simple: give a predictable path from a semester to a subject to a unit, so material is easy to find instead of scattered.

Curriculum source

The structure in this build comes from Syllabus — Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) 2026, as per NEP 2020 issued by the Pharmacy Council of India. It is used as an interim source (status: provisional) until RIPER's official syllabus for the applicable cohort is supplied and verified.

  • Authority: Pharmacy Council of India
  • Document: Syllabus — Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm) 2026, as per NEP 2020 (2026)
  • Source: https://pci.gov.in/documents/1455/B.Pharm_Syllabus_2026-_NEP2020_compressed.pdf
  • Checked: 2026-10-20

How content is handled

  • Only the official course structure is shown as curriculum; nothing is invented.
  • Study material is added subject by subject and reviewed before it appears.
  • We do not mirror copyrighted textbooks — material is linked from its lawful source.
  • This is a static site: there is no login and no shared upload database.

This is not an official publication of RIPER.
