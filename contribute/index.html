Contribute material

Have notes, a previous question paper, or a lab manual for a subject? Contributions are welcome and are reviewed before they are published.

How to send it

  • Open a GitHub issue with the subject code, semester, a link (e.g. Google Drive) or an attachment, and a note on the source.
  • Or email the maintainer via the Contact page.
This is a static site. There is no shared upload database and no login — a form here could not store submissions. So please use the issue tracker or email above, which reach a maintainer.

What we can and can't publish

  • ✅ Your own notes, openly licensed material, and links to lawful sources.
  • ❌ Copyrighted textbooks, paid coaching material, restricted institutional files, or personal data.

See CONTRIBUTING in the repository for the full guide.
