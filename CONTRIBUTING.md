# Contributing to RIPER e-Library

Thanks for helping students find useful learning resources.

## Resource suggestions should include
- title and resource type,
- semester/subject/unit mapping verified against the current official syllabus,
- language,
- original author/source and URL,
- licence/permission details,
- recent link/file check,
- accessibility or quality notes.

## Review rules
- Never submit patient, student, or confidential institutional information.
- Do not submit paid textbooks or third-party content unless redistribution is permitted.
- Curriculum labels must match the official source.
- Suggestions are not published until reviewed.
- Do not claim a form stores submissions unless it actually sends data to a monitored destination.

## Current suggested workflow
Open a GitHub issue for a non-sensitive resource suggestion or correction. A maintainer reviews source, rights, relevance, and availability before updating the public catalogue. Do not include private data or credentials in issues.
